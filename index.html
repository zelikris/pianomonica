About Mrs. Monica

Mrs. Monica brings warmth, encouragement, and joyful energy to every lesson she teaches. A classically trained pianist with a Piano Performance Diploma and more than 30 years of piano teaching experience, she is passionate about helping her students build confidence and experience the excitement of learning to play the piano.

Her credentials reflect a deep commitment to teaching. She is a member of the American College of Musicians and the National Guild of Piano Teachers, and she holds a Master of Music in Piano Pedagogy and a Master’s in Music Education, graduating with highest honors. She has also held a Preschool through 12th Grade Music Teaching Certificate for 15 years and explored advanced teaching methodologies through doctoral-level coursework in Piano Pedagogy. Her extensive background allows her to pair proven instructional methods with a warm and child-centered approach.

Her teaching combines piano fundamentals with age-appropriate music games and movement activities. Mrs. Monica believes that children learn best through play, positive reinforcement, and when lessons are fun, interactive, and filled with encouragement.  By turning learning into a fun adventure, she helps students stay motivated and excited about making music and enjoy their musical journey.

Believing that every child learns differently, Mrs. Monica adapts her teaching to each student's personality, learning style, and age. Her teaching emphasizes healthy technique, musical expression, creativity, and, most importantly, the joy of making music. She considers it a great privilege to share her love of music with each student and family she serves and looks forward to helping every child through the wonderful adventure of piano study.

Teaching Philosophy

  • Music should be joyful and accessible.
  • Technique and creativity develop together.
  • Encouragement builds confidence.
  • Every student deserves a personalized learning path.

If you’re looking for a supportive teacher who helps students thrive, Mrs. Monica would love to welcome you to her studio.

Beginner Lessons

Ideal for students with little or no experience. I help with:

  • Reading music
  • Rhythm, technique, coordination
  • Interactive musical games, iPad music apps
  • Fun, motivating songs

Intermediate & Advanced Lessons

For students who already read music and want to grow. We work on:

  • Technique
  • Expression, dynamics, articulation, phrasing
  • Music theory, harmony, analysis
  • Overcoming stage fright, memorization strategies
  • Preparation for auditions and recitals

Adult Lessons

Flexible, goal-oriented lessons for adults returning to piano or starting for the first time. Learn at your own pace with music you enjoy.

Methods

  • Faber Piano Adventures
  • Music for Little Mozarts
  • Michael Aaron’s Piano Course
  • Alfred Basic Piano
